## 🇨🇳 中文

从已核验的产品或服务事实、受众与平台目标出发，制作达人原生风格的社交视频，覆盖种草、测评、专家分享、用户证言、无脸视角、手部实测与数字人栏目等形态，产品与真人出镜都不是前提。流程先建立产品事实与素材路由，再在内部推演出 5 个观看体验确实不同、可直接进入第一镜的 0–3 秒开场候选；按需确认承载方式与人物/试穿锚，然后先写一段能连续说完的口播，再按动作、信息、视点或场景的真实变化拆分镜。跨片段同一声音用同一份 Voice Lock 保持连续，每个 clip 只为已声明的主体带对应参考图。最终产出视频素材与成片并全部落画布；不适用于品牌官方大片、连续剧情或没有行动目标的视频。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 产品事实 + 受众/平台 → 达人原生种草成片
- 内部推演出 5 个差异明显、可直接开拍的 0–3 秒开场候选
- 按需选承载方式：真人/数字人/角色/产品/手部 POV/字幕旁白
- 先写连续口播，再按动作与视点变化拆成可执行分镜
- 跨片段同一声音用一份 Voice Lock 维持连续性
- 分镜与逐字口播确认后编译生成，素材与成片全部落画布

## 🇬🇧 English

Starting from verified product or service facts, audience and platform goals, it produces creator-native social videos covering seeding, reviews, expert shares, customer testimonials, faceless POV, hands-only demos and virtual-host series — neither a physical product nor a real person on camera is required. It first builds the product fact sheet and routes the source assets, then derives five genuinely different candidates for the 0–3 second opening, each able to become the first shot directly. Delivery format and creator or try-on anchors are confirmed only when they change the outcome, after which one continuously speakable script is written and then split into shots by real changes in action, information, viewpoint or scene. A single Voice Lock keeps the same voice continuous across clips, and every clip carries refs only for the subjects it actually declares. Output is video assets plus a finished cut, all landed on the canvas; brand films, serialized drama and videos without an audience action goal are out of scope.

### ✨ What it does

- Product facts plus audience and platform in → native creator seeding cut out
- Five genuinely distinct 0–3 second opening candidates, each ready to shoot as the first shot
- On-demand delivery format: real person, digital human, character, product, hands-only POV or voice-over
- Writes one continuously speakable script first, then splits it into shots by action and viewpoint
- A single Voice Lock keeps the same voice continuous across every clip
- Compiles and generates only after shots and verbatim script are confirmed; assets land on canvas
